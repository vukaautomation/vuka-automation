    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    How long does a project take?

    Most single-process automations (Starter package) take 5-7 days. Multi-system projects (Growth) run 1-2 weeks. Enterprise builds are scoped individually after a discovery call.

    Is my data safe?

    Every project starts with a signed NDA. We only access the systems needed for your specific automation, and don't retain your data beyond what's required to build and support the solution.

    What if I don't have technical staff?

    Every package includes 30 days of post-launch support, and we document how everything works. For ongoing help beyond that, we offer optional support retainers.

    How does payment work?

    50% deposit to begin, 50% on delivery. No hidden costs — pricing is agreed upfront in the proposal.

    What tools/platforms do you work with?

    WhatsApp, email, existing CRMs, spreadsheets, and most common business software — we integrate with what you already use rather than forcing you onto new tools.

    Do you work with small businesses or only large companies?

    Both. The Starter package is built for smaller teams automating one process; Enterprise is for larger organizations needing multi-department integration.

    Are you B-BBEE compliant?

    Yes. Vuka Automation is a Level 1 B-BBEE Contributor with 135% procurement recognition. This means working with us maximizes your company's procurement scorecard.
